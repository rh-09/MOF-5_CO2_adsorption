  physical anisotropy. Longer/replicate runs are recommended before

CAVEATS FOR THE METHODS SECTION
  - Framework was treated as fully rigid/frozen; framework flexibility
    (breathing modes) can measurably affect diffusion coefficients in MOFs
    and should be mentioned as a modeling choice/limitation if not also
    tested with a flexible framework for comparison.
  - This is a single production trajectory. Reviewers will likely expect
    either multiple independent replicates (different initial velocities)
    or a substantially longer single run, with uncertainty reported across
    replicates rather than only across time-blocks of one run.
  - D reported here is uncorrected for finite-size effects; note this if
    comparing directly to experimental or other simulation literature values.
